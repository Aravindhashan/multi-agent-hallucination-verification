The Dilemma

Why Standard RAG Fails in Production

Confirmation Bias

Standard retrievers search only for words confirming the generated prompt. They never deliberately search for contradictory medical or empirical facts.

Echo Chambers & Pollution

Multiple web sources frequently repeat the exact same unverified press release. Naive counts treat 5 copies as 5 independent proofs, inflating false confidence up to 3.4x.

Syntactic Claim Fragmentation

Lists and bullet points without periods produce orphaned nouns (e.g. * Nateglinide.) that standard sentence splitters cannot verify because they have no predicate.

Decoupled Evaluation

Decoupled Hallucination vs. Coverage Gap

Metric 1

Factual Hallucination Index (H)

H = (1 / |C|) · Σ max(0.75, conf_c)

Only scales up when authoritative external evidence directly contradicts the claim.

Metric 2

Epistemic Coverage Gap (U)

U = (1 / |C|) · Σ max(0.50, P_neutral,c)

Measures absence of evidence without falsely penalizing unverified facts as lies.
